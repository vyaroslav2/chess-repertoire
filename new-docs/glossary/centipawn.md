**Centipawn (`cp`)** — one hundredth of a pawn. It is the unit engines use to say who is better and by how much. 100 cp is roughly one pawn up.

`cp` is always from White's point of view. A negative `cp` is good for Black.

**Stored** as a whole number of centipawns: `35`, `-120`, `1000`.

**Shown** in pawns, with a sign and two decimals: `+0.35`, `-1.20`, `+10.00`. This is the usual chess notation.

|Stored (`cp`)|Shown (pawns)|Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|`0`|`0.00`|Equal|
|`35`|`+0.35`|White slightly better|
|`-120`|`-1.20`|Black better by about a pawn|
|`300`|`+3.00`|White up about a piece|
|`-1000`|`-10.00`|Black winning|

A mate is not a `cp`. It goes in `mate` instead ([[DB|DB.12]], [[DB|DB.14]]).
