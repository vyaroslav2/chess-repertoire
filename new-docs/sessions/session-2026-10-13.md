## Decisions
- The answer's eval shows once the move is played or revealed. A wrong move shows none.
- Evals beyond ±9.99 keep two decimals (`+12.34`). A mate shows as on Lichess: `#3` (White mates), `#-3` (Black mates).
- Zero shows as `+0.00`, always with a sign.
- Since Stockfish 15.1, 100 cp no longer means "a pawn up": at `+1.00` White wins about half the time in Stockfish's own games. The glossary no longer reads evals as material.
- Glossary: "eval" is its own entry, not part of "centipawn": it covers more than the unit.
- Tag style: `reviewed`, not `"#reviewed"` (38 files against 2).
- Large coordinates are off-white, not white: pure white glares on the near-black page. `#e0dcd6` is Material Design's 87% white for high-emphasis text, in the warm tint of the opening name.
- Evals are hidden by default: they spoil Anki-style cards. A press shows them for the current card only, so it never spoils the next. No "only after the answer" mode: the answer's eval already waits, and the rest is YAGNI.
