## Done
- Rewrote [[UI]], the board screen spec: measured the fonts, colours and sizes of every text, then cleaned it up as blocks UI.20–UI.29. The old UI.01–UI.18 were dropped; their numbers are not reused.
- Self-hosted the fonts with `next/font`: Inter had been asked for but never loaded, so text fell back to Segoe UI and Arial. The browser no longer contacts Google.
- Centred the board down the page as well as across, rounded to whole screen pixels at 125%. Moved the temporary status line into the free space left of the board.
- Built temporary test pages to compare fonts at their real size and colour: `/fonts` (opening name), `/coords`, `/moves` and `/grid` (the real move list with 200 hard-coded moves, plus a mock with evals).
- Measured Lichess on lichess.org: coordinates 12px Noto Sans bold, sized by window width; move list in Noto Sans, numbers 14px centred in a shaded cell with no dot, moves 16.59px; evals inside the move cell at 0.8em, Roboto weight 300, `#949494`.
- Found why Inter looked looser than in Obsidian: Obsidian draws Inter at 30px, where Inter's optical size switches to its tighter display design. At 14px both are identical.
- Restyled the move list: Roboto for numbers and moves, numbers centred in a flush shaded column without the dot, Lichess's sizes, and the panel widened from 188px to 220px so the longest moves fit.
- Checked the database for evals: only Black's moves have them (11 of 11); White's have none (0 of 36), and the engine cache holds none of their resulting positions.

## Decisions
- Opening name: Inter 14px, letter spacing -0.0062em (Inter's recommendation for 14px), `#979490` (6.05 : 1).
- Coordinates: Arial 13px, weight 400, `#817e7a` (4.52 : 1), with Arimo as a self-hosted stand-in where Arial is missing.
- Move numbers and moves: Roboto, one font for both, as Lichess does. Numbers 14px, centred, no dot, in a shaded column (`#33312e`) running flush top to bottom. Moves 16.59px.
- Move list panel: 220px.
- Evals: in the move's cell, right-aligned, JetBrains Mono 0.8em, weight 300, `#949494`, two decimals (-9.99 to +9.99). Lichess's cell padding (0.5em, 0.3em). The panel grows to 318.4px when they arrive.
- New colours meet the 4.5 : 1 contrast guideline; the move numbers on the shading (2.9 : 1) are still open.
- Judge type at its final size, not zoomed: zoomed comparisons misled the letter-spacing choice.
