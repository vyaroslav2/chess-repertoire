### A4.01 — Included White moves with their reasons, threat/trap flags, and popularity - from A3

**What the code does**  
A4 begins with the list of White moves that survived A3's filter.

Each included move already carries the information produced in [[A3.05]], including:

- its SAN;
    
- its inclusion reason;
    
- its Master Threat flag;
    
- its Amateur Trap flag;
    
- its probability value.
    

Nothing is calculated or changed at this box.

The code simply reaches:

`for (const whiteMove of whiteCandidates)`

and begins working through the included White moves one by one.

So A4.01 is the receiving side of the hand-off from [[A3.08]].

The next box is [[A4.02]], which checks whether any included White moves remain to be processed.

**Why this matters**  
This box marks the boundary between selecting White moves and actually covering them.

A3 decides which White moves deserve inclusion.

A4 takes those already-selected moves and works through them individually.

The filtering decisions are therefore not repeated here. A4 receives the result of A3 and uses the reason, flags and probability that are already attached to each move.

**Why it may have been designed this way**  
Likely: this box exists mainly to make the entry point into A4 explicit.

There is no separate A4.01 operation in the code because A3 and A4 are sections of the same generator function.

The diagram boundary makes the responsibility clearer:

A3: "Which White moves do we cover?"

A4: "For each included White move, how do we add and continue that line?"
