### A2.03 — Above 2%?

**What the code does**  
The generator checks whether the current position's cumulative probability is greater than `0.02`, meaning greater than 2%.

This is a strict "greater than" comparison.

So:

- above 2% -> yes;
    
- exactly 2% -> no;
    
- below 2% -> no.
    

If the answer is yes, the algorithm goes to [[A2.05]] and gives the position a provisional depth budget of 15.

If the answer is no, it goes to [[A2.04]] and checks the next probability threshold. The diagram shows these same two branches.

**Why this matters**  
This check separates the highest-probability branches from the rest.

Positions above 2% are allowed to enter the largest of the three probability-based depth-budget groups.

The boundary itself matters: a position carrying exactly 2% does not get the 15-move budget. It continues to the next test instead.

However, this box does not by itself determine how deep the generator will actually go.

After the probability-based budget is chosen, [[A2.08]] takes the smaller of that budget and the depth limit supplied by the caller. The current caller supplies 3, so at present a 15-move provisional budget is reduced to 3 before the depth check is made.

So the 2% distinction exists in the algorithm, but with the current caller setting it does not change the effective depth limit.

**Why it may have been designed this way**  
Likely: 2% was chosen as the boundary for branches considered common enough to justify the largest depth budget.

The broader design is to let more probable branches continue further than less probable branches.
