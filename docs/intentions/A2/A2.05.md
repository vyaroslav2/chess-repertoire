### A2.05 — budget = 15

**What the code does**  
If the current position's cumulative probability is at least 2%, the generator sets its probability-based depth budget to `15`.

In the code, the variable is called `dynamicMaxDepth`.

It starts at `5`, but the successful branch from [[A2.03]] changes it to `15`.

Nothing is stopped or expanded at this box.

The value `15` is only a provisional depth budget. The algorithm next goes to [[A2.08]], where this budget is compared with the depth limit supplied by the caller.

**Why this matters**  
This is the largest of the three probability-based depth budgets.

A position reaches this box only if its cumulative probability is at least 2%, so the algorithm is prepared to let a relatively common branch continue as far as 15 full moves.

However, `15` is not necessarily the depth the generator will actually use.

[[A2.08]] takes the smaller of this value and the caller's depth limit.

At present the caller's limit is 3, so the effective limit becomes 3 rather than 15. The A2 diagram explicitly notes that the current caller limit makes all three probability-based budgets ineffective at present.

**Why it may have been designed this way**  
Known: branches at or above 2% are considered common enough to justify the largest depth allowance.

The broader intention appears to be that common branches may be explored further than rare branches, while still allowing an overall caller-supplied limit to cap the amount of work.
