### A3.08 — Print how many White moves survived and add that number to the running White-moves-found total. Pass the included moves, with their reasons, flags, and popularity, to A4 to cover one by one

**What the code does**  
This box is reached when [[A3.06]] finds that at least one White move survived the filter.

The generator prints:

`Found X White threats/mainlines to process.`

where `X` is the number of surviving White candidates.

It then adds that number to the running `totalWhiteMovesFound` counter.

For example, if:

- `totalWhiteMovesFound` was `12`;
    
- this position has `3` surviving White candidates;
    

then the counter becomes `15`.

The generator then loops through the surviving candidates one by one.

Each candidate still carries the information produced by [[A3.05]], including:

- its SAN;
    
- its inclusion reason;
    
- its Master Threat flag;
    
- its Amateur Trap flag;
    
- its probability value;
    
- the Masters and Amateur statistics returned by the filter.
    

The next part of the algorithm, A4, processes each surviving White move separately.

**Why this matters**  
This is the point where A3 finishes selecting White moves and hands the surviving set to the part of the generator that actually covers them.

`totalWhiteMovesFound` counts moves that survived the A3 filter, not every move originally returned by the human databases.

So if A3.04 found ten distinct moves but A3.05 kept only three, this box adds `3`, not `10`, to the running total.

The surviving candidates are not merged together after this point. A4 processes each White move individually, so each can lead to its own repertoire branch and Black response.

The printed wording `"White threats/mainlines"` is only a log label. The surviving list can also contain moves carrying the current Amateur Trap classification.

**Why it may have been designed this way**  
Likely: the running counter gives a simple measure of how many White moves the generator has actually decided to cover, rather than how many raw moves it encountered.

Passing the complete filtered candidate onwards also avoids recalculating its reason, flags and probability later in A4.
