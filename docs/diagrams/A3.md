Work these out first:

1. Popularity = this move’s amateur games ÷ total amateur games here.
2. Master Threat flag? — at least 15 master games AND smoothed master White score ≥ 58%.
3. Floor bump — if the Master Threat flag is set and popularity is under 1%, set popularity to exactly 1%.
4. Amateur Trap flag? — at least 15 amateur games AND smoothed amateur White score ≥ 58% AND the Master Threat flag is not set.
5. The bar — 5% at moves 1–4, 10% at moves 5–8, 15% thereafter.

Then decide whether to include the move and set its reason, in this order — first match wins:

1. Popularity at or above the bar → include; reason: Mainline.
